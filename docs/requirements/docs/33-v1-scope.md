# V1 Scope

## Included

### Customer
- Product browsing
- Search
- Filters
- Sorting
- Product details
- Variants
- Guest checkout
- Email/password
- Phone OTP
- Profile
- Addresses
- Cart
- Wishlist
- Orders
- Tracking
- Reviews
- Notifications
- Enquiries
- B2B quotation interaction

### Seller
- Registration
- KYC/document submission
- Admin verification
- Product/catalogue upload
- Manual product submission
- Listing workflow
- Listing revision history
- Seller confirmation/change requests

### Admin
- Dashboard
- Product management
- Categories
- Brands
- Variants
- Dynamic attributes
- Seller verification
- Listing workflow
- Inventory
- Orders
- Customers
- Sellers
- Payments
- Refunds
- Reviews
- Enquiries
- Quotes
- Delivery
- Blogs
- SEO

### Commerce
- B2C
- B2B
- Commission rules
- Platform fees where applicable
- Delivery margin
- Cashfree
- UPI
- UPI QR
- Net Banking
- Credit Card
- Pay on Delivery

### Delivery
- Pune
- Pimpri-Chinchwad
- Same-day where eligible
- Seller/pincode/product/inventory-based rules
- Ground-level unloading/placement limitation
- Pickup where configured

### SEO
Strong technical/product/category SEO foundation.

## Explicitly Excluded
- Interior service booking
- Partial payment
- EMI
- Coupons
- Banners as a dedicated admin module
- Android app
- iOS app
- Multi-city operations
- Multiple warehouses as an active V1 operational feature

Architecture must remain future-ready for the roadmap.

Confirmed V1 additions: Admin-created categories; seller-managed inventory; Seller + Admin product images; B2B business-profile verification; configurable tax/GST calculation with transaction-level snapshots; empty-project Replit initialization. DashDecor remains a temporary working name.
