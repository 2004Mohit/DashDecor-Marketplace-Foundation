# Product Requirements

## Product Categories
The initial catalogue should follow the categories identified in the Executive Summary reference:
- Civil & Interiors
- Furniture / Hardware
- Electrical
- Plumbing
- New Launches
- Tools

The catalogue must be extensible for additional interior and construction-material categories.

## Product Data
Products must support:
- Name
- Description
- Brand
- Category/subcategory
- Images
- Variants
- Specifications
- Price
- GST/tax information
- Stock/availability
- SKU/reference information where applicable
- Product attributes
- FAQs and useful product content
- Seller association

## Product Variants
Products may have variants such as:
- Size
- Thickness
- Colour
- Finish
- Material
- Other category-specific attributes

## Pricing
Pricing must support configurable platform rules and transparent customer-facing calculations.

## Product Availability
Availability can depend on:
- Seller
- Pincode
- Inventory
- Delivery capacity
- Product type
- Delivery schedule

## Product Reviews
Eligible customers can review products/orders. Admin must have moderation/control capabilities.
