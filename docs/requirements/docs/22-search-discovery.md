# Search and Discovery

## V1
Implement practical, fast and maintainable search/discovery.

## Search
Support:
- Product name
- Brand
- Category
- Relevant product attributes
- SKU/reference where available

## Browsing
Support:
- Category pages
- Subcategories
- Brand pages/filtering
- Product listing pages
- Product detail pages

## Filters
Support relevant:
- Price
- Brand
- Category
- Product attributes
- Availability
- Seller where appropriate
- Pincode/serviceability where appropriate

## Sorting
Basic:
- Relevance
- Price low-to-high
- Price high-to-low
- Newest
- Popular where reliable data exists

## Avoid
Do not over-engineer AI recommendations or advanced personalization in V1.

## Pincode
Where relevant, use customer pincode/location to determine availability and delivery eligibility.
