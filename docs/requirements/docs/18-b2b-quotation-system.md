# B2B Quotation System

## Purpose
Support customers who need bulk/interior-material orders that may require negotiated pricing rather than standard retail checkout.

## Users
B2B customers may include:
- Contractors
- Interior designers
- Architects
- Builders
- Businesses
- Other approved professional buyers

## Enquiry
Customer can submit:
- Products
- Quantities
- Required delivery location
- Preferred delivery timing
- Notes
- Project information where applicable

## Quote
Admin/relevant authorized users can:
- Create quote
- Add/remove items
- Change quantities
- Apply negotiated prices
- Apply delivery charges
- Apply platform fees where applicable
- Add notes
- Set validity period

## Revision
Quote must support revisions and a history of previous versions.

## Customer
Customer can:
- View quote
- Accept
- Request changes
- Reject/decline
- Continue to payment/order when accepted

## Future
Architecture should allow:
- B2B pricing tiers
- Repeat orders
- Saved project material lists
- Credit terms
- GST invoice workflows

## Confirmed Decisions Update

Verified B2B capabilities require an approved business profile. Collect configurable business/legal information, address, contact details, GSTIN where applicable and supporting documents; Admin controls approval/rejection/suspension.
